Aberdeen Praya Road: SUMO calibration and validation

Results browser for sumo-signal-demo. SUMO model of a 1.6 km corridor in Aberdeen, Hong Kong, with demand from Transport Department detector counts on 22 and 23 September 2026. Data: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), Transport Department via DATA.GOV.HK.

Study area

Detectors (blue eastbound, orange westbound) and signals (dark squares: junctions, light: pedestrian crossings).

Observed and simulated counts

15-min counts as hourly rates; median seed of 5. Open circles: observed count built from fewer than 8 of 30 periods.

Calibration grid

Mean share of 15-min comparisons with GEH < 5 over 5 seeds (lcKeepRight = 0). Uncoloured cells: at least one run failed the validity rule, so the set cannot be selected. Box: selected set.

Signal plans

Delay per vehicle including time waiting to enter, 07:00-10:00, mean of 5 seeds with 95% CI. Hatched bars: no run passed the validity rule (queues back up out of the network), so these delays are not comparable. Signal timings in operation are not public, so all plans start from SUMO-generated phases.
